Task 3 – Deep Dive Analysis & Interactive Dashboard

📌 Business Problem
Which book genres drive higher reader engagement and satisfaction?

📊 KPIs Defined
- Total Books – Total number of books in dataset
- Total Reviews – Measure of reader engagement
- Average Rating – Indicator of customer satisfaction
- Average Price – Pricing trend across books

🔍 Deep Dive Analysis
Performed genre-based analysis to understand:
- Engagement patterns across genres
- Relationship between price and ratings
- Distribution of books across price categories

📈 Dashboard Features
- Interactive slicer (Genre filter)
- KPI cards for key metrics
- Scatter plot for price vs rating analysis
- Price category distribution visualization
- Matrix summarizing genre performance

💡 Key Insights
- Fiction & Action dominates reader engagement
- Price has minimal impact on ratings
- Most books fall under medium price category
- Ratings remain consistent across genres

🛠 Tools Used
- SQL (Data Analysis)
- Power BI (Dashboarding)

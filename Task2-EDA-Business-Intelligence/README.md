Exploratory Data Analysis & Business Intelligence
Book Dataset Analysis (Task 2 – Internship Program)

📌 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a book dataset to uncover meaningful patterns, trends, and relationships. The analysis focuses on answering business-oriented questions using SQL and presenting insights through a structured Power BI dashboard mock-up.

📊 Dataset Overview

The dataset contains structured information about books, including:
- Book Name
- Author
- Genre
- Price
- Rating
- Reviews Count
- Print Length
- Publishing Year
- Reading Age
The data was cleaned and prepared before performing analysis.

❓ Business Questions Addressed

- Which genres generate the highest total reviews?
- What is the average rating across different genres?
- Is there a relationship between price and rating?
- Do books with more reviews have higher ratings?
- How has book publishing trended over time?
- Does book length influence average rating?

🔍 Key Findings

- Fiction & Action genres dominate in total review count.
- Average ratings across most genres remain consistently high (above 4.4).
- Price shows almost no correlation with rating.
- Higher review count does not necessarily indicate higher rating.
- Short books slightly outperform long books in terms of average rating.
- Publishing activity has increased significantly in recent years.

📈 Analysis Performed
1 Descriptive Statistics
- Average Rating
- Average Price
- Total Reviews

2 SQL-Based Business Queries
- Aggregations using GROUP BY
- Filtering and sorting
- Correlation calculations
- Category-based comparisons

3 Multivariate Analysis
- Price vs Rating Correlation
- Reviews vs Rating Correlation
- Genre-wise performance comparison

4 Static Dashboard Mock-up
A KPI-focused dashboard was designed in Power BI including:
- Average Rating
- Total Reviews
- Average Price
- Reviews by Genre
- Rating by Genre
- Publishing Trend Over Time
